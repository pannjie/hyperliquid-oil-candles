Hyperliquid oil markets over the weekend of 28 Feb – 1 Mar 2026

Three oil perpetual markets kept trading on Hyperliquid while real oil futures were closed for the weekend, during military action in the Middle East and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. Each section shows 1-minute candles for Friday evening and Saturday morning.

xyz:CL (trade[XYZ], WTI)

The biggest of the three markets. With real oil futures shut for the weekend, the price was held by a +5% cap at 70.654 from Saturday 14:13 to Sunday 23:00 UTC, then jumped to 75.73 when trading resumed, setting off a burst of short liquidations.
